# Fictional demo returns policy — version 1

Returns: Customers may request a return within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Proof of purchase is required. Final-sale items cannot be returned. This demo cannot determine a specific order's eligibility because delivery date and item details are unavailable.

Refunds: After a returned item passes inspection, a refund is issued to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. This is a fictional policy, not a promise about a real order.
